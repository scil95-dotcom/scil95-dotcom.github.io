Risk events you can audit.

A personal, AI-assisted Python demo. Prior-history signals, explicit execution costs and cash constraints produce a reproducible audit trail. The invented fixture includes a price gap that closes the position and halts further entries.

What this demonstrates

  • Timing: strategy signals at bar i read prices only through i − 1.
  • Controls: cash covers purchase plus fee; holdings cannot exceed the absolute unit cap.
  • Traceability: every fill records its reason, execution price, fee and resulting cash balance.
  • Reproducibility: Decimal amounts remain strings in JSON; rerunning the fixture produces identical output.

Model assumptions and reproduction
  • signal: prior window mean; prior last price above mean means long
  • execution: current quote, adverse slippage on both sides
  • fees: basis points of execution notional on each fill
  • position cap: absolute units, no shorting or leverage
  • quantity increment: 0.000001
  • decimal precision: 50
  • stop: quote at/below entry execution price * (1 - stop_loss); halt entries
  • terminal: liquidate any remaining position at final quote with costs
  • model limits: no liquidity, latency, spread, taxes or corporate actions model
